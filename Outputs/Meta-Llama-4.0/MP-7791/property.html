Test Properties for TC-001

Business Impact

  • Impact on Business Operations: The search functionality in the Recipients field is critical to the Notifications widget, allowing users to efficiently find and select recipients for notifications. If this test fails, it may hinder the ability of users to send notifications to the intended recipients, leading to delays or missed notifications.
  • Potential Business Consequences: If this test fails, the potential business consequences include:
    • Delayed or missed notifications, which may impact business operations and decision-making.
    • Decreased productivity and efficiency due to manual workarounds or increased time spent searching for recipients.
  • Criticality to Business Processes: This functionality is critical to the business process of sending notifications and ensuring that the right people are informed in a timely manner.

Risk Analysis

  • Risks Addressed: This test case addresses the risk of the search functionality not working as expected, which may lead to errors or inefficiencies in the notification process.
  • Severity of Risks: The severity of this risk is high, as it may impact the ability of users to send notifications and may lead to delays or missed notifications.
  • Likelihood of Risks: The likelihood of this risk occurring is medium, as it depends on the complexity of the search functionality and the quality of the implementation.

Complexity

  • Complexity Factors: This test case is complex due to the need to verify that the search functionality works correctly for different types of recipients and that the results are grouped correctly.
  • Technical Challenges: The technical challenges addressed by this test case include ensuring that the search functionality is efficient and accurate, and that it can handle large datasets.
  • Dependencies: This test case depends on the correct implementation of the search functionality in the Notifications widget, as well as the availability of test data and the correct configuration of the widget.

Potential Failure Consequences

  • Potential Failure: If this test fails, it may indicate that the search functionality is not working correctly, which could lead to errors or inefficiencies in the notification process.
  • Downstream Impacts: The downstream impacts of this failure could include:
    • Delayed or missed notifications
    • Decreased productivity and efficiency
    • Potential impact on business decision-making
  • Recovery Requirements: To recover from this failure, it would be necessary to identify and fix the root cause of the issue, which may require additional testing and validation to ensure that the search functionality is working correctly.
